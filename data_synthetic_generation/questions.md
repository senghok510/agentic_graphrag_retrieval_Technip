1. Obligation ↔ Penalty reasoning

What penalties apply if the contractor fails to meet delivery milestones, and how are these penalties calculated based on delay duration?
Under which conditions can penalties be waived if the contractor demonstrates partial compliance with obligations?
How does the contract define the relationship between performance obligations and penalty thresholds?

2. Client ↔ Contractor interactions

What are the respective responsibilities of the client and contractor in case of a delay caused by incomplete client inputs?
How does the contract allocate liability when both client and contractor contribute to a failure?
In what cases can the contractor suspend work due to client non-compliance, and what are the consequences?

3. Payment ↔ Milestones ↔ Delay

How are payments linked to milestone validation, and what happens if a milestone is delayed or rejected?
What conditions must be met for the contractor to still receive partial payment in case of delayed deliverables?
How does the contract define the impact of delayed payments by the client on delivery obligations?

4. Termination ↔ Breach ↔ Penalties

Under which conditions can the client terminate the contract due to contractor breach, and what penalties apply?
What is the financial impact of early termination initiated by the contractor due to client default?
How are penalties and outstanding payments handled in case of contract termination?

5. Risk ↔ Liability ↔ Force majeure

How does the contract distinguish between force majeure events and contractor liability in case of delays?
What obligations remain enforceable during a force majeure event, and which penalties are suspended?
How is liability redistributed when a third-party dependency fails?

6. Change management ↔ Cost ↔ Schedule

How are contract modifications handled when changes impact both cost and delivery timelines?
What approval process is required for scope changes, and how does it affect penalties and obligations?
How does the contract ensure alignment between revised milestones and payment schedules?

7. Multi-clause reasoning (more advanced)

If a delay is caused by both a subcontractor failure and a client approval delay, how are responsibilities and penalties distributed?
How do payment suspension clauses interact with performance obligations and termination rights?
What is the sequence of actions when a breach triggers both penalty clauses and termination clauses?

8. Scenario-based (very good for RAG evaluation)

If the contractor delivers late due to delayed client feedback, but partially meets technical requirements, what penalties apply and what payments are still due?
In a situation where force majeure delays delivery but the client incurs financial losses, how does the contract resolve liability and compensation?
If a milestone is rejected due to quality issues but later corrected, how are penalties and payments recalculated?
