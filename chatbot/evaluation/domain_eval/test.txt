Change Orders are formal written instructions issued by the Company 
to modify the scope, cost, or schedule of the Works. For Re-measurable 
Works, pricing is based on actual quantities measured during execution, 
using pre-agreed Unit Rates or Time Rates as specified in the contract. 
When a Change Order impacts Re-measurable Works, the pricing is adjusted 
using the applicable rates from the contract schedules (e.g., Schedule B1 
or C1). If no applicable rates exist, new rates are negotiated based on 
the principles used to establish the original rates. Additionally, indirect
costs are only applied if the Change Order results in an approved extension 
of time to the critical path of the Work Time Schedule. The adjustments 
from Change Orders are incorporated into the progress measurement system 
and invoicing, ensuring alignment with the updated scope and quantities.
